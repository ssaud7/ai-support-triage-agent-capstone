# Disputing a Charge on Your Invoice

If a customer believes they were charged incorrectly, they should first check their
invoice history in Account Settings > Billing before contacting support, since most
discrepancies are due to prorated upgrade charges or annual renewal charges.

To formally dispute a charge, the customer must submit a dispute within 60 days of
the charge date. Disputes submitted after 60 days cannot be processed through
standard support and must go through the customer's card issuer directly.

Each customer account is flagged internally after their second dispute within a
12-month period. Accounts with 2 or more prior disputes require a supervisor's
manual review before any further billing adjustment is approved, since repeated
disputes may indicate a payment method issue rather than a billing error.

Disputes are typically resolved within 5 business days.
