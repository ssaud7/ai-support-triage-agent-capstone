# Billing Cycle and Invoice Dates

All subscription plans bill on a monthly cycle that starts on the date the customer
originally signed up. For example, a customer who subscribed on the 12th of the month
will be billed on the 12th of every subsequent month.

Invoices are generated automatically 3 days before the billing date and emailed to the
address on file. Customers can view all past invoices at any time from the "Billing"
tab in account settings.

If a billing date falls on the 29th, 30th, or 31st and the current month is shorter,
the charge is moved to the last calendar day of that month instead.

Annual plans are billed once per year on the signup anniversary date and receive a
15% discount compared to paying monthly.
