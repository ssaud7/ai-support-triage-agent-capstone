# Upgrading or Downgrading Subscription Plans

Customers can change plans at any time from Account Settings > Billing > Change Plan.

**Upgrades** take effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount for
the remainder of the current billing cycle, calculated as:
`(new plan price - old plan price) / days in cycle * days remaining`.

**Downgrades** take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately.
This means customers keep access to their current plan's features until the cycle
ends, and there is no partial refund issued for downgrading mid-cycle.

Customers cannot switch directly from a monthly plan to an annual plan mid-cycle;
they must wait until their next renewal date, at which point the option appears
automatically in the billing portal.

Switching plans does not reset usage counters (API calls, storage, seats) — those
continue to accumulate across the change.
