# Refund Eligibility Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund if they request it within 30 days of the
original charge date. This applies to both monthly and annual plans.

After 30 days, refunds are only granted in exceptional cases, such as a documented
service outage that significantly affected the customer's usage, or a billing error
caused by our system.

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method only. We cannot issue refunds
as store credit, to a different card, or via a different payment method than the one
originally charged.

Partial refunds may be issued for annual plans canceled mid-term, calculated as a
prorated amount for the unused portion of the year, minus any discount that was
applied for choosing the annual plan.
