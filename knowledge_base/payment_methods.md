# Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods for all subscription plans:

- Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit or debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (mobile app only)
- ACH bank transfer (available only for annual Enterprise plans)

We do not accept wire transfers, cryptocurrency, gift cards, or checks.

Customers can update their payment method at any time from Account Settings >
Billing > Payment Methods. Changing a payment method does not trigger an immediate
charge — the new method will be used starting with the next scheduled billing date.

If a customer's card expires, they will receive an email reminder 7 days before the
next billing attempt asking them to update their payment details.
