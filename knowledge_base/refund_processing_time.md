# How Long Refunds Take to Process

Once a refund is approved, it is issued immediately on our side, but the time it
takes to appear in the customer's account depends on their payment method:

- Credit or debit card: 5–10 business days
- PayPal: 1–3 business days
- ACH bank transfer: 7–14 business days

Customers should be told to check with their bank if a refund has not appeared after
the maximum window, since banks occasionally hold processed refunds for an extra
billing cycle before displaying them.

Refund confirmation emails are sent the moment a refund is approved, including a
reference number the customer can provide to their bank if needed.
