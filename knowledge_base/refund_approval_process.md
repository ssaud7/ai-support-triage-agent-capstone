# Refund Approval Process

Refunds under $100 are approved automatically by the support system once eligibility
is confirmed (see refund_eligibility.md).

Refunds of $100 or more require manual approval from a human supervisor before they
are issued, regardless of how clear-cut the eligibility case is. This is a hard rule
and applies even to long-tenured customers or documented outages.

The approval step must happen before any refund is marked as processed in the system
— a refund should never be communicated to the customer as "done" until a human has
explicitly approved it.

When escalating a refund for approval, include: customer ID, order ID, refund amount,
reason for the refund, and the customer's dispute history if any.
